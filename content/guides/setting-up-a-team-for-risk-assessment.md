Teams on the Risk site are not set up there. They come from TRUST. This guide is for Tech Risk. It covers where teams come from, the one list you do keep, and what to check before a team starts.

## Where teams come from

A group on the Risk site is a TRUST team. TRUST decides the teams with its team grouping setting: either product families, or each L3's organization from HR. The Risk site follows whichever is on, and reads TRUST's result. Nothing is worked out twice.

A team's people, its lead and their count come from TRUST for its newest month. Teams are not added, renamed or retired on the Risk site. A team TRUST no longer has drops out of the lists. Assessments saved under an old group that is not a TRUST team are kept, but the group is no longer listed. Its results page says so.

Until TRUST holds teams, the Groups page says so plainly.

## The sign-off approver

The one thing kept on the Risk site for a team is its sign-off approver. Open Edit, then the "Sign-off approvers" list. Each TRUST team has one entry. Type the approver's SAM account, add the reason and save. Leave it empty for none.

The approver signs off when the L3 over the team's lead can't be found. The approver may also sign off at any time. See [Changing the rating settings and lists](/guides/changing-the-rating-settings-and-lists/) for how editing works.

## What to check before a team starts

1. **The team is listed.** Open the Groups page as Tech Risk. The team's card should show its status and how many people it has.
2. **It has a lead.** The card reads "TRUST team led by" and the lead's name. Without one, nobody can find the L3, and only Tech Risk (and the approver) can sign off.
3. **The L3 can be found.** After the team's first submit, the sign-off box on its results page names the L3, or says it could not find one. If a team has several leads, they should all sit under one L3.
4. **An approver is set** if the L3 can't be found, or if you want a second person who can sign off.
5. **The questions are there.** The questionnaire is built from the risk files and the Questions list. If it says there are no questions yet, check those first.
6. **The people can open it.** The team's people, the managers above them, Tech Risk, auditors and leadership can see the team. Only the team's people, managers above and Tech Risk can answer. Only the lead, managers above and Tech Risk can submit.

> **Example.** A team's card says "TRUST team · 9 people", with no lead. Tech Risk checks the team's owners in TRUST first. Until a lead is found, Tech Risk sets an approver for the team.
