After a team submits its risk questionnaire, someone senior to the team looks at the assessment and signs it off, or sends it back. This guide is for L3s and the people they name to sign off for them.

## Who signs off

The L3 over the team's lead signs off. The site finds the L3 from the HR data, the same way it finds the team and its lead. These people can sign off or send back:

- the L3 over the team's lead,
- the delegate the L3 has named,
- the team's approver, who is set by Tech Risk,
- Tech Risk.

The sign-off box on the results page says who should sign off. If the site cannot find the L3 (for example, a team has no lead, or its leads sit under different L3s), the box says so. The approver signs off instead, and if there is no approver, Tech Risk does.

Only the newest assessment can be signed off, and only while it shows Submitted. If answers changed after the submit, the team is a Draft again and needs to submit first.

## Name a delegate

An L3 can name one person to sign off in their place. In the sign-off box, open "Name someone to sign off for" your name, type the person's SAM_ACCOUNT from HR, and choose Save. Choose Clear to remove the delegate. Tech Risk can do this too.

A delegate covers every team under that L3. Each change is kept, and the newest one stands.

## Sign off or send back

Open the team's results page and read the assessment. Then, in the sign-off box, write a short note (up to 500 characters) and choose one of two buttons:

- **Sign off.** The team's status becomes Signed off. The note is kept with your name and the time.
- **Send back.** Use this when the answers need another look. **The note is the reason the team sees**, so say what you would like them to look at.

A note is needed for both. The sign-off box and the history show who decided, when, and the note, to everyone who can open the team.

After a send back, the team can answer and submit again. It stays Sent back until it does. Submitting again makes a new assessment, and the earlier ones stay in the history.

> **Example note when sending back.** "Three drivers are not answered yet. Please answer them and submit again."

## The "also changed answers" flag

If the person signing off also saved answers in this assessment's period (after the team's earlier assessment, up to this one), the sign-off carries a flag. It reads like "[Name] also changed 2 answers in this assessment."

The flag never stops a sign-off. It is there so everyone can see it, on the results page and in the history. People are matched by SAM_ACCOUNT. If you answered some questions and you are also the one signing off, a second pair of eyes may be worth it.

## If you cannot sign off

If the sign-off box says you can't sign off, it names who can. If you think you should, Tech Risk can help.
